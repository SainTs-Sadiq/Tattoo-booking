Before you book

How to book

Fill out the booking form or DM @sadsaints.tats.pa on TikTok. Send your idea, placement, size, and reference images. We reply with availability and the deposit link.

Pricing

Pricing depends on size, detail, and placement. Send your idea through the form and you will get a quote before you pay anything.

Deposit & rescheduling

A $100 non-refundable deposit is taken to hold your date. Reschedule at least 1 week before your appointment, and your deposit is honored for 4 months from the day you pay it.
